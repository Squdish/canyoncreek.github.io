ABOUT THE TERRITORY

Built for settlers who take the story seriously

Canyon Creek sits where the river bends under the shadow of the old mining ridge — a town built by settlers, held together by uneasy law, and shaped entirely by the people who live in it.

We run a whitelisted, heavy-roleplay server. That means every rancher, outlaw, lawman, and shopkeep on our land was screened in before they got a saddle. It keeps the town honest, and it keeps the stories good.

— The Canyon Creek Territory Office
