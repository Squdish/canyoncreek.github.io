Before you file

A few things the Territory Office looks for in a strong application:

  • A character concept with a real reason to be in Canyon Creek — not just a name and a hat.
  • Familiarity with our roleplay standards, especially value of life and fear roleplay.
  • Honest, original writing. We can tell when a backstory was copied.
  • Patience — every application gets read, but review takes time.
